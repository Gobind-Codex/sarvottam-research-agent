### 2. Bull Case  

| Argument | Evidence |
|----------|----------|
| **Strong top‑line momentum** – revenue up double‑digits YoY and order book expanding > 20 % YoY【S3】. |
| **Data‑centre demand** – data‑centre cables now 9 % of domestic sales and management expects to double this in two years【S2】. |
| **Capacity uplift** – new Bharuch plant adds ~30 % cable capacity and should start commercial production in Q3 FY27【S3】, supporting growth. |
| **Export tailwinds** – export share rose to 21 % of revenue【S3】, diversifying demand. |
| **Leverage remains modest** – net‑debt‑to‑equity 0.35× and most capex funded internally【S2】, limiting financing risk. |
| **Pricing power on copper** – two‑thirds of contracts have price‑variation clauses; management expects to pass higher copper costs to customers over the next two quarters【S3】【S2】. |
| **Improving governance** – promoter pledge fell to 4.1 % of promoter holding by mid‑2026【S8】, mitigating earlier concerns. |

---

### 3. Bear Case  

| Risk | Evidence |
|------|----------|
| **Margin compression** – operating margin fell 170 bps to 11.2 % due to a ~14 % rise in copper prices【S1】; lag in price‑adjustment clauses could keep pressure on earnings. |
| **Rising receivable days** – days sales outstanding increased to 96 days, driven by large state‑utility customers【S2】, raising cash‑collection risk. |
| **Tax litigation** – GST demand of ₹46.3 cr could become material if the appeal fails, despite current management view【S7】. |
| **Historical promoter pledge** – 2024 report of 35 % pledge raised governance red flags【S4】; even if reduced, the prior level may signal underlying financing stress. |
| **Capex intensity** – ₹620 cr capex for Bharuch plant, with 30 % of capacity yet to be commercialised, could strain cash flow if demand softens. |
| **Competitive pricing pressure** – management says competitors are not pricing irrationally, but any price war could further erode margins【S2】. |
| **Unrelated regulatory penalty** – a fine on a similarly named local cable‑TV operator (₹8 lakh) highlights potential brand‑reputation exposure, though not directly linked to the listed firm【S6】. |

### 4. Open Questions  

1. **Which revenue figure is correct for Q1 FY27?** The company filing shows ₹1,248 cr, while a news outlet reports ₹1,428 cr. Clarification is needed.  
2. **When will the copper price‑adjustment clauses fully offset higher input costs?** Management expects two quarters, but timing is uncertain.  
3. **What is the detailed composition and maturity profile of the term debt used for Bharuch capex?** Only “some term debt” is mentioned.  
4. **How material could the ₹46.3 cr GST demand become if the appeal is unsuccessful?** Management downplays impact, but cash‑flow implications are unclear.  
5. **Will the promoter pledge remain low, or could it rise again under financing needs?** Recent filing shows low pledge, but prior high levels raise the question of future changes.  
6. **What is the expected trajectory of receivable days as utility payments normalize?** No concrete plan disclosed.
